About Nexus World

A company built on a decade of hands-on delivery

Nexus World Company Limited is a Saudi civil construction and electromechanical company based in the Eastern Province. We cover precast ready-made buildings, general building, M&E works, heavy equipment rental and process instrumentation — backed by leadership that has personally delivered hospital, residential and industrial projects across Khobar, Dammam and Jubail.

We're built to work the way industrial and commercial clients expect: reliable scheduling, safety-first site management, and manpower that's already familiar with the standards of Saudi Arabia's major operators.

  • 100% Saudi-based company serving the Eastern Province
  • Leadership experienced with SABIC, Saudi Aramco & Royal Commission-grade standards
  • Full-scope delivery from foundation to instrumentation commissioning
What we do

Our seven core line services

From groundwork to commissioning, Nexus World covers the full scope of build, fit-out and technical support that industrial and commercial clients in Saudi Arabia rely on.

01

Precast Ready-Made Buildings

Factory-produced precast units delivered, erected and finished to compressed schedules, with repeatability of quality on every block.

02

Civil Construction

Full-scope civil construction for hospitals, residential compounds and industrial facilities, from foundation to finishing.

03

Mechanical & Electrical Works

Design, installation and commissioning of mechanical systems and electrical infrastructure for commercial and industrial sites.

04

Manpower Services

Vetted engineers, supervisors and tradesmen for oil & gas, petrochemical, offshore, power and desalination projects.

05

Heavy Equipment Rental

Access to plant and heavy equipment for erection, piping and structural works, sized to your project timeline.

06

Start-Up & Maintenance

Continuous maintenance services for process control instrumentation, plus site safety programs and inspection support.

07

Process Instrumentation & Integration

Installation of pneumatic and electronic instrument devices, process skid fabrication, and hydraulic/pneumatic control systems.
