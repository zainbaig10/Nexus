About Nexus World

A company built on a decade of hands-on delivery

Nexus World Company Limited is a Saudi civil construction and electromechanical company based in the Eastern Province. We cover general building, M&E works, heavy equipment rental and process instrumentation — backed by leadership that has personally delivered hospital, residential and industrial projects across Khobar, Dammam and Jubail.

We're built to work the way industrial and commercial clients expect: reliable scheduling, safety-first site management, and manpower that's already familiar with the standards of Saudi Arabia's major operators.

  • 100% Saudi-based company serving the Eastern Province
  • Leadership experienced with SABIC, Saudi Aramco & Royal Commission-grade standards
  • Full-scope delivery from foundation to instrumentation commissioning
